## 2. Regulatory Stance & Banking Disclaimers

1. **Non-Banking Simulator:** VOUT is a standalone budgeting tool and simulated transaction organizer. It is not an FDIC-insured institution, electronic money institution (EMI), or payment service provider.
2. **Offline-First Data Sovereignty:** The application operates completely client-side. No consumer banking credentials or sensitive payment details are requested or transmitted.
3. **Open Standards:** Built adhering strictly to open web and mobile guidelines under React Native and Expo frameworks.
